About StarLoom

Bringing the night sky closer to everyone.

Our Story

StarLoom Observatory started with a simple idea: everyone should get the chance to see the universe clearly. What began as a few friends sharing a telescope on weekends has grown into a team that hosts guided night-sky tours, rents professional equipment and teaches astrophotography.

We choose dark-sky locations far from city lights, so you can see planets, star clusters and the Milky Way with your own eyes.

What We Stand For

🔭 Our Mission

To make astronomy simple, exciting and open to people of every age.

🌌 Our Vision

A community where looking up at the stars is a shared everyday experience.

⭐ Our Values

Curiosity, safety, honesty and respect for the natural night sky.
